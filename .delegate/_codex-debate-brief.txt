CLAUDE'S POSITIONS (respond to each):

1. SECTION ORDER: Talent500Section (a "join before launch, first 500 get perks" urgency/promo banner)
   renders immediately after HeroSection, before CategoriesSection or FeaturedTalentsSection — i.e.
   before the visitor has seen any real talent, portfolio evidence, or social proof. Claude's position:
   this front-loads a sales/urgency pitch ahead of credibility-building content, which is backwards for
   a cold visitor; it should move lower (e.g. after FeaturedTalentsSection or TestimonialsSection) once
   trust signals have landed.

2. HERO SEARCH REMOVED BUT MARKUP KEPT: `SHOW_HOME_SEARCH = false` disables the hero search bar
   entirely (comment says "temporary kill switch") but the full form markup, state, and submit handler
   remain dead in the component. Claude's position: (a) as UX, a marketplace hero without a search
   entry point pushes all discovery onto two link buttons, which is a weaker affordance for someone
   who already knows what they want; (b) as code hygiene, ~30 lines of live-but-unreachable form
   markup/handlers sitting behind a hardcoded boolean is dead-weight that should either be restored or
   deleted, not parked indefinitely.

3. AUTO-SCROLLING CATEGORY CAROUSEL: CategoriesSection auto-scrolls an infinitely-looping category
   rail (pauses on hover/focus, respects prefers-reduced-motion). Claude's position: ambient auto-moving
   carousels are a well-known low-engagement pattern — content changes on its own schedule, not the
   user's, and a moving target is harder to click precisely. A static grid (or manual-only rail) would
   serve better despite the engineering effort invested in a seamless loop.

4. GUEST PRICE BLUR: TalentCard blurs the price (CSS-blurred, not hidden) for guests, unless the
   price is already "on request." Claude's position: this is a defensible growth lever (creates a
   reason to sign up) but also a trust cost — visitors comparison-shopping can't see real numbers and
   may read it as evasive, especially on a page whose own copy elsewhere emphasizes "no hidden fees."

5. TALENT500 STYLING BREAKS LOCAL CONVENTION: Talent500Section is written entirely in raw inline
   `style={{...}}` objects (including a `color-mix()` gradient) even though every sibling section in
   this same file uses the CSS Module (`styles.xxx`). Claude's position: this is inconsistent with the
   file's own established pattern (not the global codebase pattern, which does favor inline styles) —
   it should be moved into LandingPage.module.css to match its neighbors.

6. PAGE LENGTH / SCROLL DEPTH: the page stacks ten full sections (Hero, Talent500, Categories,
   FeaturedTalents, Workflow, Campaign, Feature, Testimonials, FAQ, FinalCTA) before any secondary
   page. Claude's position: this risks scroll fatigue and dilutes the primary conversion actions;
   Feature and FAQ in particular could move to a dedicated page or be trimmed, keeping the homepage
   focused on proof (talents, testimonials) plus one clear CTA path per audience (talent vs brand).

7. CAMPAIGN SECTION MIXES TWO INTENTS: CampaignSection puts a gallery of approved brand-moment photos
   next to an unrelated dark "post a job" CTA panel in the same two-column section. Claude's position:
   combining social-proof gallery and brand-acquisition CTA in one section dilutes both messages; they
   read as two different jobs-to-be-done bolted together for layout convenience.
